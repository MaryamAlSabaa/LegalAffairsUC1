# Internal legal review test pack

All organizations, terms and reference positions are fictional. Do not sign or use these documents for real procurement. No real personal data is included.

1. Create a test request and upload `sample-research-services-agreement.pdf`. Suggested title: TEST - Research analytics services. Description: Review a proposed analytics supplier agreement involving student identifiers and research data. Select the closest available services/contracts category.
2. As Legal Reviewer or Legal Manager, open Internal legal review > Prepare.
3. For a template comparison, add `comparison-template-test-only.txt` as reference text, title it "Synthetic research services reference v1 - TEST ONLY", and confirm approval only as a simulated sandbox action. This is not an approved KU template. Alternatively run without a template and verify no template match is claimed.
4. Run AI review with configured Gemini access. Review findings and page references; model output may vary.
5. In Prepare response, edit the draft and use Requester preview. Verify sharing is disabled until the response is nonempty and confirmation is checked. Editing text must clear confirmation.
6. As the requester, verify draft findings and templates are unavailable. Only deliberately confirm and share using a test requester account; then verify that account sees only the confirmed response.

## Expected review observations (test expectations, not legal advice)

- Page 1: Parties and dates are explicit; 120-day opt-out renewal, unilateral price increases, undefined acceptance criteria, full advance payment and unapproved extra fees deserve review.
- Page 2: Personal data categories are identified; no clear data protection obligations, incident notice, hosting restrictions or deletion period. Weak confidentiality survival, supplier-owned outputs, unilateral publication rights, a very low liability cap, one-sided indemnity and undefined service levels deserve review.
- Page 3: Asymmetric termination, no refund or data export, unresolved governing law/forum, unilateral website amendments and blank approvals/signatory authority deserve review.
- Checklist: Some criteria should be supported, some should need review. Do not expect every checkbox to be selected. Missing insurance requirements can be flagged for relevance review; do not assume all missing terms are legally mandatory.
- The synthetic template should reveal deviations in renewal, payment, data handling, ownership, publication, termination and change control.
- Classification should suggest an agreement/services category, summary should describe the supplier engagement, and the draft response should request clarifications without claiming legal approval.
